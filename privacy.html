隐私政策 · 时刻保鲜

最后更新：2026 年 4 月 20 日

感谢你使用「时刻保鲜」（以下简称"本 App"）。我们非常重视你的隐私，本政策说明我们如何处理信息。

1. 我们收集什么

我们不收集、不储存你的任何个人数据。你在本 App 中录入的所有物品信息（名称、日期、分类、备注等）默认全部存放在你设备本地。

若你主动开启「iCloud 同步」，这些信息会通过 Apple iCloud 在你自己的账号内加密同步，用于在你的多台设备间共享和备份。数据保存在你个人的 iCloud 空间中，只有你本人可以访问，我们无法读取。关闭该开关即可停止同步。

2. 权限说明

  • 相机：仅在你主动使用扫码或拍照录入功能时调用，用于识别商品条码或拍摄物品照片。照片和识别结果仅保存在你本地设备，我们不会上传。
  • 通知：用于发送你设置的到期提醒。所有提醒在设备本地生成与触发。

3. 第三方服务

本 App 不集成任何广告 SDK，不做跨应用追踪，也不使用广告标识符（IDFA）。

为了了解哪些功能真正被用到、用户在哪一步遇到困难，本 App 集成了 TelemetryDeck 匿名使用统计。它上报的内容仅限于：

  • 匿名的功能使用事件，例如「添加了第一件物品」「打开了升级页」；
  • 基础运行环境信息：App 版本、系统版本、设备型号、语言与地区；
  • 一个匿名设备标识：由系统提供的「供应商标识符」经加盐 SHA-256 哈希后生成，用于区分不同设备的使用情况。它不是广告标识符（IDFA），无法反推到你本人，你删除本应用后该标识即失效。

其中不包含你的物品名称、日期、照片、备注等任何内容，也不会与你的身份关联、不会用于广告投放或跨应用追踪。TelemetryDeck 数据托管在欧盟境内并遵守 GDPR。

订阅与内购功能由 Apple App Store 提供，相关交易信息由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理，我们无法访问你的支付信息。

4. 数据安全

由于数据仅存于你的设备本地，请自行做好设备备份与保护。卸载 App 将会删除所有已录入的数据。

5. 儿童隐私

本 App 适用于所有年龄（4+），不专门面向儿童，也不会收集任何可识别儿童身份的信息。

6. 政策更新

若未来我们需要更新本隐私政策，将在本页面公布变更内容，并在 App 内提示。

Privacy Policy · FreshDue

Last updated: April 20, 2026

Thank you for using FreshDue ("the App"). Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how your information is handled.

1. What We Collect

We do not collect or store any of your personal data. All the items you enter in the App (name, dates, categories, notes, etc.) are stored locally on your device by default.

If you turn on iCloud Sync, those items are synced through your own Apple iCloud account so they stay available and backed up across your devices. The data lives in your personal iCloud storage, only you can access it, and we cannot read it. Turning the switch off stops syncing.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: Used only when you actively use barcode scanning or photo input. Photos and recognition results stay on your device. We never upload them.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver the expiry reminders you configure. All reminders are generated and triggered locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The App contains no advertising SDKs, performs no cross-app tracking, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

To understand which features people actually use and where they get stuck, the App includes TelemetryDeck for anonymous usage analytics. It reports only:

  • anonymous feature events, such as a first item being added or the upgrade screen being opened;
  • basic environment information: app version, OS version, device model, language and region;
  • an anonymous device identifier, derived from the system vendor identifier via salted SHA-256 hashing, used to tell devices apart. It is not the advertising identifier (IDFA), cannot be traced back to you, and stops being valid once you delete the App.

It never includes your item names, dates, photos, or notes, is not linked to your identity, and is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. TelemetryDeck hosts its data in the EU and is GDPR compliant.

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store. Transaction data is processed by Apple under its own privacy policy; we have no access to your payment details.

4. Data Security

Because your data is stored only on your device, please back up your device as appropriate. Uninstalling the App will delete all the data you entered.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is intended for all ages. It does not target children specifically and does not collect any information that could identify a child.

6. Policy Updates

If we update this privacy policy in the future, we will post the changes on this page and notify users within the App.
